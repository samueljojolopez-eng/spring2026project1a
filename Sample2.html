The Hidden Persona

This piece represents how one person can have various "masks" that others know you by, while never really seeing who is underneath. A study on the layers of human identity.

Vastness & Perspective

Instead of focusing on the person, the focus is on the vastness of the sky. Sometimes you just need to be alone to truly take in the view. This features my "self-inserts"—characters that allow me to express myself in a way words cannot.

Violets of the Night

A test on drawing specific angled perspectives looking up at the night sky. I used purples and shades of violet to give a sense of calm, showing that even at night, there is light.

Wacky Calm

Experimenting with wacky colors while still portraying calm and wonder. While I usually use darker purples and pinks, I decided to play with lighter colors here to practice pure technique.

Automotive Study

A technical test for drawing cars and unique shadow placement. This piece allowed me to play with high-contrast color choices and how lighting interacts with mechanical surfaces.

Atmospheric Lighting

A return to darker settings to test how well I could portray lighting and cast shadows in a night scene, ensuring the atmosphere remains heavy but the details stay visible.
